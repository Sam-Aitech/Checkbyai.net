What is a COS Check?

A COS check is the process of verifying the authenticity of a Certificate of Sponsorship document. This verification ensures that the COS document is genuine, issued by a licensed sponsor, and hasn't been tampered with or forged.

Red Flags in Fake COS Documents

  • Incorrectly formatted reference numbers
  • Invalid sponsor license information
  • Suspicious employment periods
  • Metadata inconsistencies
  • Poor document quality or formatting
