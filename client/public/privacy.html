Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you use CheckByAI — including our UK Sponsor Licence Monitoring service and our Certificate of Sponsorship verification tool.

Information We Collect

Documents You Upload

When you use our verification service, you may upload:

  • Certificate of Sponsorship documents (PDF, images, or other formats)
  • Related immigration documents for verification

Important: Uploaded documents are processed for verification and automatically deleted after analysis is complete. We do not permanently store your documents.

CoS Check — What We Do and Don't Store:
  • The CoS Check tool analyses only the PDF document structure and metadata to detect whether the file has been digitally altered or edited.
  • We do not extract, read, store, or share the textual or visual content of any Certificate of Sponsorship.
  • We do not share CoS data with any third party, including the Home Office, UKVI, or Companies House.
  • The original document is permanently deleted from our servers immediately after analysis — typically within 60 seconds.

Technical Information

We automatically collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Usage data (pages visited, verification timestamps)
  • Device and operating system information

Account Information (if applicable)

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username and password (encrypted)
  • Verification history (metadata only, not documents)

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide verification services: Analyze uploaded documents for authenticity
  • Improve our AI: Enhance detection accuracy (using anonymized data only)
  • Prevent fraud: Identify and prevent abuse of our service
  • Communicate with you: Send verification results and service updates
  • Comply with legal obligations: Respond to lawful requests from authorities

Companies House Data

To enrich sponsor profiles on our platform, we query the Companies House public register API — the official UK government database of registered companies — using the sponsor's company name as a search term.

We retrieve only publicly available fields from the Companies House register:

  • Company registration number
  • Company registration status (e.g. Active, Dissolved)
  • Company type
  • SIC codes (Standard Industrial Classification)
  • Incorporation date
  • Registered office address
  • Date of last filed accounts

The retrieved fields are stored in our database and displayed on sponsor profile pages within CheckByAI to help users assess the legitimacy of a licensed sponsor. No other use is made of this data.

We do not share any data with Companies House. We only read from the public register. No user data, no CoS data, and no personal information is submitted to or shared with Companies House in any form. The data we retrieve is already publicly available on the Companies House website.

Lawful basis: We process this publicly available data under Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR (legitimate interests) — specifically, to provide accurate sponsor intelligence to our users and to help employers and immigration professionals verify the registration status of UK licensed sponsors.

Data Retention

Key commitments:
  • Uploaded documents are deleted immediately after verification (typically within 60 seconds)
  • Verification metadata (date, result, file type) is retained for 90 days
  • Account information is retained until you delete your account
  • No personal data from documents is extracted or stored

Data Security

We protect your information using:

  • Bank-grade encryption (AES-256) for all data transmission and storage
  • Secure servers with regular security audits
  • Access controls limiting who can view uploaded documents
  • Automatic document deletion after processing

See our Data Security page for comprehensive security information.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Third-party processors who help us operate our service (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request
  • Safety: To prevent fraud, abuse, or harm to our service or users

We never share uploaded documents or personal immigration information with marketing companies, data brokers, or unauthorized third parties.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling essential cookies may affect service functionality.

Automated Decision-Making

Our service uses artificial intelligence (AI) to produce verification results — classifying documents as Genuine, Suspicious, or Fake — based on forensic analysis of metadata and document structure. These results are automated and may be significant to your immigration decisions.

Your rights regarding automated decisions (UK GDPR Article 22):
  • You have the right to request a human review of any automated verification result
  • You have the right to contest an automated result you believe is incorrect
  • You can request that a member of our team manually reviews your case
  • Automated results are based on technical metadata only and do not constitute legal or immigration advice

To contest an automated result or request a human review, contact us at support@checkbyai.net with your verification reference number. Our admin team is authorised to override AI findings where appropriate.
