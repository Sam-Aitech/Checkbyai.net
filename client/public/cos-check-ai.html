What is a Certificate of Sponsorship?

A Certificate of Sponsorship is an electronic record, not a physical document, issued by UK employers through the UK Visa & Immigration Sponsorship Management System (SMS). It contains a unique reference number and details about your job and sponsor.

The CoS is essential for applying for UK work visas, including Skilled Worker visas and other sponsored work routes. Legitimate sponsors receive their CoS reference via the official Home Office system.

Risks of Fake or Invalid Certificates of Sponsorship

Unfortunately, visa scams involving fraudulent CoS documents are increasingly common. The consequences of using a fake or invalid Certificate of Sponsorship include:

  • Visa refusal: Your visa application will be rejected if the Home Office cannot verify your CoS
  • Financial loss: You may lose visa application fees, healthcare surcharges, and any payments made to fraudulent agents
  • Immigration consequences: Submitting false documents can lead to bans from the UK
  • Job offer scams: Fake sponsors often demand upfront fees for non-existent positions
  • Identity theft: Scammers may collect your personal information for fraudulent purposes
Warning signs of CoS scams: Be cautious if an "employer" asks for money upfront, sends a PDF instead of an official SMS notification, or cannot provide verifiable company registration details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I check a CoS on the UKVI website?

The UK Visas and Immigration website does not provide a public tool for applicants to verify CoS documents. Only licensed sponsors can access the Sponsorship Management System. Our AI tool provides an independent preliminary check.

Does an AI CoS check replace legal advice?

No. Our tool provides a technical analysis of document authenticity but does not constitute legal or immigration advice. If you have concerns about your CoS, consult a registered immigration advisor or solicitor regulated by the OISC or SRA.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use bank-grade encryption to protect all uploaded documents. Your CoS information is analyzed securely and is not shared with third parties. Documents are automatically deleted after verification.

How can I verify my sponsor is legitimate?

Check the UK government's register of licensed sponsors. Legitimate sponsors must be on this list. Also verify the company exists on Companies House.

What should I do if the AI flags my CoS as suspicious?

Do not proceed with your visa application. Contact your sponsor directly using independently verified contact details (not those provided in the suspicious document). Consider seeking advice from a regulated immigration professional. Report suspected fraud to Action Fraud.
