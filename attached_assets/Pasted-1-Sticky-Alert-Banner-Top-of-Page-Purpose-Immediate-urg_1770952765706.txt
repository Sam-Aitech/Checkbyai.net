Key Visual Conversion Rules (for your implementation)

Color Psychology: Use amber/gold for the "FREE" elements (caution, temporary). Use solid black or deep navy for the "PAID" elements (authority, safety). Red is reserved only for the "REVOKED" status and urgency banners.
Typography: The price "£24.99" should appear smaller than the outcome "Keep Your Visa." People buy the outcome, not the subscription.
Whitespace: Pack the free search tool with data (dense, informative). Give the paid tiers generous whitespace (premium, breathable).
Lock Icons: Use lock icons on all free-tier limitations to create mild frustration that converts.
The "Trap" Mechanism
Ensure the free search result page explicitly shows what the user is not getting:

"Status: Active" (Green check)
"History: [Locked — Upgrade to view]"
"Alert Setting: [Locked — Upgrade to enable]"
"Next Check: Manual only"
This creates loss aversion (they see the value is there, they just cannot access it) rather than simple absence of value.

Priority for Max Revenue:
Build the Pricing Section and the "Lock" UI on free results first. These two elements alone will convert browsers to subscribers before you even build the rest of the page.
