Optimising for “AI search” (ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini, etc.)

You can’t “SEO hack” AI models, but you can make your site the most attractive source to quote.

6.1. Make your pages AI-friendly

AI systems prefer:

Clear, fact-based sections with headings.

Bullet lists / checklists that are easy to quote.

Explicit statements like:

“To check if your UK CoS is genuine, you should: 1) … 2) … 3) …”

Up-to-date info referencing official sources.

6.2. Provide unique value AI models don’t already have

Models are already trained on generic advice. Your differentiator:

Specific fraud patterns you see in fake CoS (anonymised).

Data like: “In our internal checks, X% of suspicious CoS had these 3 issues…”

Visual patterns (e.g. wrong sponsor licence format, layout issues).

This kind of specific, real-world detail makes AI tools more likely to surface and link to you when browsing.
